==================================================
Theoretical Analysis: Loop Invariants in Selection Sort
==================================================

1. Loop Invariant:
   At the start of each iteration i, subarray A[0...i-1] contains the i 
   smallest elements of the array in sorted order.

2. Why loop runs for (n-1) elements:
   Once (n-1) elements are placed in sorted positions, the remaining 1 element 
   is naturally the largest and is already in its correct final spot.

3. Time Complexities:
   - Worst-Case: Theta(n^2)
   - Best-Case:  Theta(n^2) (No improvement because minimum search always scans all remaining elements)
